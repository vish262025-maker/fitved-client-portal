Prenatal & Pregnancy Yoga FAQs

When can I safely start prenatal yoga during my pregnancy?

Most expectant mothers begin prenatal yoga in their second trimester (week 13 onwards) with OB/GYN clearance. Gentle trimester-specific breathing, pelvic floor stabilization, and strain-relief stretches prepare your body for a safe delivery.

Does prenatal yoga help with morning sickness and back pain?

Yes. Gentle lateral side stretches, cat-cow spine mobilizations, and breathwork help reduce lower back pressure, ease morning nausea, and improve sleep quality throughout pregnancy.

Postnatal & Postpartum Core Recovery FAQs

When can I start postnatal exercise after a normal delivery vs C-section?

Typically 6 weeks post normal delivery and 8–10 weeks post C-section, following your doctor's clearance. FitVed trainers guide progressive core stabilization before introducing heavier workouts.

How does FitVed help heal Diastasis Recti (abdominal separation)?

Our certified postpartum specialists use targeted transverse abdominis (TVA) activation, breath coordination, and pelvic floor strengthening drills to safely close abdominal separation without high-risk crunches.

Health Conditions, Clinical Rehab & Back Pain FAQs

How does FitVed assist software engineers with chronic back pain and tech neck?

We combine spinal decompression yoga, deep core stabilizer activation, and shoulder blade mobility drills specifically targeting 8+ hour desk-sitting postural strain.

Can strength training lower HbA1c levels for type-2 diabetes?

Yes. Progressive resistance training increases skeletal muscle glucose uptake, improving insulin sensitivity. Many FitVed clients see measurable reductions in HbA1c alongside fat loss under medical supervision.

Can FitVed work directly with my doctor's or surgeon's discharge notes?

Yes. We specialize in doctor-aligned clinical fitness for post-knee replacement, spinal rehabilitation, and cardiac health, adapting every exercise to your surgeon's clearance notes.
