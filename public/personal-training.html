FAQs — Personal training in Bangalore

How much does a personal trainer cost in Bangalore?

Personal training typically costs ₹8,000–₹18,000 per month depending on session frequency (2–5 times/week) and format (1-on-1 or small group). FitVed offers a free trial session before any commitment.

What does a personal training session include?

Each session includes a warm-up, customized strength/cardio workout based on your goals, cool-down with stretching, and progress tracking. Your trainer also provides nutrition guidance and weekly body composition reviews.

Can seniors or people with health conditions use a personal trainer?

Yes. FitVed specializes in medically-informed training for seniors (55+), diabetics, BP patients, arthritis sufferers, and post-surgery recovery. All programs are designed around your medical history.

Do I need gym equipment at home?

No. Your trainer uses your society gym equipment or brings portable equipment (resistance bands, dumbbells, mats). Many effective exercises use just bodyweight.

How is a personal trainer better than a gym membership?

A personal trainer gives you customized programming, proper form correction, injury prevention, accountability, and zero-commute convenience. At a gym, you're on your own with generic routines.
